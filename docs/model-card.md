## Intended use

Detection of small unmanned aerial vehicles in RGB imagery for perimeter
awareness, research and demonstration.

## Out of scope

- Targeting or any autonomous engagement decision.
- Certified use in regulated airspace or safety-critical operations.
- Night/thermal operation (RGB only).
- Ranges beyond the training distribution.

## Known failure modes (pre-registered)

- **Small targets (<32 px).** Recall degrades sharply with distance; AP-small is
  reported separately and must not be hidden behind overall mAP.
- **Birds of prey at altitude.** Primary false-positive source for any RGB
  counter-UAS detector; see the bird FPR number.
- **Occlusion, rain, heavy motion blur.** Underrepresented in training data.
- **Multiple simultaneous UAVs.** IoU tracker has no appearance model, so
  identity is not preserved through occlusion.

## Data provenance

Datasets are not redistributed. Each source and its license is recorded in
`benchmarks/results.json`. Research-only datasets were used for evaluation only;
weights derived from them carry that restriction forward.

## Governance behaviour of the demo

Every inference emits a hash-chained audit record containing model id, weights
hash, input hash, policy verdict and retention TTL. Raw frames are never
persisted; EXIF/GPS is removed before decode. Synthetic (mock) replies are
labelled and can never return a `deny` verdict.
